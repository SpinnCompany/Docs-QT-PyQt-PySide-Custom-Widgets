A widget gallery tells you what the parts look like. The new
[App showcase](/Usage-Examples/AppShowcase) shows the machines: **92 complete,
runnable applications** built with Custom Widgets, every screenshot captured
from the app itself — not a mockup.

<!-- truncate -->

## What's in it

Dashboards for finance, crypto, cash flow, smart-home control and e-commerce
analytics. A three-pane chat client with voice messages and reactions. A music
player with a cover-flow hero. A node-graph creative studio with a code editor
and timeline. Operations consoles, agenda boards, starters — and roughly
seventy single-widget demos, each the shortest runnable answer to "how do I
wire this up".

![AuroraChat](/img/showcase-apps/aurorachat.png)

Every card lists the widgets the app actually uses (detected from its real
imports, so the list can't lie), and every screenshot clicks to full size.

## Free apps, and supporter flagships

The single-widget demos, the starters, and teaching apps like GlassHome stay
free in the repo — they are documentation. The **twelve flagship
applications** (marked **PATREON** on the showcase) ship their full source to
supporters: six dashboards, AuroraChat, two operations decks, the jobs table,
NodeStudio and RhythmoTune. Each is a production-shaped codebase — Designer
forms, compiled sources, themed stylesheets, managers and workers — meant to
be the starting point for your own app.

![SmartHomeDashboard](/img/showcase-apps/smarthomedashboard.png)

Screenshots of everything stay public. The source of the twelve is the
supporter perk.

## How to get them

Support the project on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/spinntv) or grab a
plan at [customwidgets.org/pricing](https://customwidgets.org/pricing/) —
supporters get the premium example apps alongside the Pro widget package
(DataTable Pro's virtualization, grouping, pivot and export). Tutorials and
walkthroughs land on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@SpinnTV).

Everything else — the 164-widget library, the 82 public examples, the whole
documentation site — is free under GPLv3, and
[2.3.0 is on PyPI now](/blog/custom-widgets-2-3-0-released).
