# Widget gallery

164 widgets, grouped by the job you are doing rather than by where they live in the source tree. 63 of them animate here, showing the interaction rather than a frozen frame. 23 ship in [Pro](https://customwidgets.org/pricing/); the rest are free under GPLv3.

## Complete applications

Every widget below also exists inside a real app — dashboards, chat, a music player, a node editor and more, each a runnable example. Browse all of them in the [App showcase](05-Usage-Examples/AppShowcase.mdx).
